# Product Context

## Platform
web

## Stack
- React 19 with Vite
- React Router for public and admin routes
- Framer Motion and Lenis for motion and scrolling
- Turso-backed event data for the event management surface

## Users
Primary users are HITU students who are considering joining IEEE HITU Student Branch. They arrive while exploring student opportunities and need to understand the branch, find a relevant committee or activity, and submit their interest.

Existing members and other visitors are secondary users who need public access to branch information, committees, events, gallery content, resources, and contact paths.

## Product Purpose
The website is the recruitment-first digital home for IEEE HITU Student Branch. It should help HITU students move from discovery to a clear, low-friction first step toward participation.

## Positioning
The product presents IEEE HITU Student Branch as a structured student community connecting HITU students with technical learning, committees, events, and the wider IEEE network. The meaningful position to preserve is the bridge between campus participation and a global engineering community.

## Operating Context
- The app is a responsive web experience served from a Vite development/build workflow.
- The public site includes the home, about, join, committees, membership, events, gallery, and contact routes.
- An admin surface exists for event management and should remain distinct from the public recruitment journey.
- Current joining and contact submissions are placeholder/local flows until a production backend is established.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Preserve IEEE HITU Student Branch naming and institutional context: Heliopolis International Technological University.
- Preserve the existing IEEE logo and branch imagery as product assets.
- Keep recruitment calls to action discoverable and understandable on mobile and desktop.
- Keep forms labeled, keyboard-usable, readable, and responsive.
- Do not imply that placeholder local submissions are a production joining system.

## Brand Commitments
- Use the IEEE HITU Student Branch identity consistently.
- Preserve IEEE terminology where it is factual, including committees, membership, events, IEEE Xplore, and student branch.
- Keep the site authoritative, technically credible, and student-oriented.

## Evidence on Hand
- Repository copy identifies the site as the official IEEE student branch at Heliopolis International Technological University.
- Existing routes cover recruitment, branch information, participation, and event administration.
- Existing assets include the IEEE HITU logo, committee/board imagery, gallery content, and web app icons.

## Product Principles
- Make the next participation step obvious.
- Explain branch structure before asking for commitment.
- Connect local HITU activity to IEEE's broader engineering community.
- Treat public information and administrative tooling as separate experiences.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Accessibility is a durable requirement: preserve keyboard access, explicit form labels, readable contrast, responsive layouts, and clear focus/interaction states as the product evolves.

## Build Path
comp-first
