## Design Context

### Users
This site is for visitors exploring the IEEE HITU Student Branch, including students, members, and people interested in IEEE resources and membership. They are trying to quickly understand the branch identity, credibility, and available opportunities.

### Brand Personality
Professional, credible, and academic. The interface should feel clear, trustworthy, and structured, with the IEEE blue palette carrying most of the visual identity.

### Aesthetic Direction
Light mode, high-clarity presentation, and restrained geometric accents. Use existing IEEE-inspired blues and clean typography. Avoid playful or overly decorative treatments that weaken the institutional tone.

### Design Principles
1. Keep branding explicit and easy to recognize.
2. Preserve a clean visual hierarchy in major landing sections.
3. Reuse existing assets and color tokens before introducing new visual language.
4. Prefer simple, polished emphasis over heavy decoration.
